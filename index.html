Learning aids
Appearance
Dark mode is easier on the eyes at night, and makes the photos stand out. “Device” follows your phone or computer; “Day / night” goes light in the morning and dark in the evening.
Translate this page
German, Italian, French and Spanish are hand-translated; the rest use Google Translate — imperfect, but enough to make the app usable in many languages. Scientific species names are never translated. Pick English to switch back instantly.
